Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Carly handles information on this website. Carly is a small studio, and our website is a front-end demonstration storefront.

Front-end demo website

This website is a front-end demonstration. Forms on the website collect demo information only and are processed entirely in your browser. There is no backend server, and no database is used to store any information you enter.

Cart and preferences

Your cart is stored in your browser's localStorage under the key carly_cart. Cookie banner preferences are stored in your browser's localStorage. These values stay on your device only.

PayPal payments

Payments on the checkout page are handled by the official PayPal JavaScript SDK. PayPal manages its own login, security and processing on its own systems. Carly does not collect, see, or store your PayPal login credentials or password.

Card information

Carly does not collect, request, or store any card information, including card number, expiry date, CVV, Visa, Mastercard, debit card or credit card details.

Contact form

The contact form is a front-end demo. Information you enter is not transmitted to a backend server by this website.

Updates

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in the website. The current version is always available on this page.
