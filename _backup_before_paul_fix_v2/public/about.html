About PAUL LAWRENCE JR LISTER

The studio behind the rooms you love.

PAUL LAWRENCE JR LISTER is a small atelier focused entirely on the living room — its decor, its air, its light, and the smart pieces that make it feel calm and contemporary. We curate air purifiers, ambient lighting, smart aroma diffusers, decorative smart lamps, minimalist table decor and comfort-focused devices that fit how people actually live.

Our Mission

To make the smart living room feel quietly human.

We believe technology should soften a room, not stiffen it. Our mission is to bring together the best decorative objects and smart home upgrades into a single, considered collection.

Design philosophy

Objects with the patience of craft, the precision of engineering, and the warmth of materials chosen by hand.

Smart living room vision

Air purifiers, ambient lighting, intelligent aroma and smart comfort devices that disappear into your daily life.

Selection standards

Every product is tested in our studio for noise, finish, durability and how it ages in a real living room.

Quality & usability

If it is not pleasant to touch, easy to live with, and beautiful to keep, it does not make our collection.

Customer support

Slow, careful and personal. We confirm every order by hand and answer every message ourselves.

Lifestyle curation

We curate for a life lived in the living room — reading, hosting, resting, watching, breathing.
